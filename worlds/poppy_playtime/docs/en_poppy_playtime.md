# Poppy Playtime

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

The Tapes, Entering of Rooms, and Completion of Puzzles or other Story Events are all randomized

## Which items can be in another player's world?

The items that can be in another player's world are certain room unlocks, the grabpack and its upgrades, and (at some point) enemy traps

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

When the player receives an item it will either go into the player's in game inventory or automatically apply like the grabpacks
